Steer Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Steer is an iPhone app operated by Isaac Swedberg. This policy describes the current app's handling of information and these support pages.

Information on your device

Steer stores your app selections, saved groups, routine schedules, daily budgets, preferences, subscription-access status and recorded focus intervals in a shared on-device container used by the app and its extensions. History is limited to the latest 2,000 records. This information may be included in your device backup. Steer has no user account or developer-operated backend and does not upload your app selections or focus history to us.

Apple's Screen Time tools

With your permission, Steer uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity tools to select apps and websites, apply restrictions, monitor chosen schedules or daily limits, and display usage reports. Selections are represented using Apple's tokens. Steer does not transmit these selections or reports to a developer-operated server. You can revoke Screen Time access in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

Apple processes optional subscriptions through your Apple Account. Steer uses StoreKit transaction information, such as product identifiers, expiry and revocation status, to determine access. We do not receive your payment-card details through Steer. Apple may provide developers with sales, subscription and app-performance reports through its services. Apple's handling of account, payment and diagnostic information is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your Apple settings.

Notifications and exports

Optional end-of-session reminders are scheduled locally through iOS. When you export history, you choose where to save or share the file. A service you choose to receive that file handles it under its own privacy practices.

No advertising or behavioral tracking

The app contains no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. We do not sell your app selections or focus history, or use them to track you across other apps and websites.

Support and website visits

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond to and resolve your request, retaining correspondence as needed for support and applicable obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about website visits under its privacy statement. These pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising, or a contact form.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete saved groups and routines, clear completed history, export history, and change permissions in Steer or iPhone Settings. Remove the app to remove its local app data; copies in device backups or exported files are managed separately through the relevant service or device controls. Revoke Screen Time access before removing the app if you want to release its restrictions. Removing the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when Steer's data practices change. The effective date identifies this version. For privacy questions, email isaacswedberg@gmail.com.
