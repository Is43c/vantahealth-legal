Steer Support

Help with Steer, the iPhone app for quick blocks, routines and focus history.

Email isaacswedberg@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Steer version, and what happened. Share screenshots only if you are comfortable with the information they contain. Never send your Apple Account password or verification codes.

Start a block

Open Block, choose the apps you want to block, choose a duration, and tap Block now. Saved app groups let you reuse a selection. Scheduled routines and daily budgets are separate from quick blocks.

Screen Time access

Steer needs your permission to apply restrictions through Apple's Screen Time tools. Connect Screen Time in Steer's Settings and confirm the system request. If access was revoked, reconnect before starting a block. App selection and actual restrictions require a physical iPhone; simulator previews do not block real apps.

Always a way out

In Steer, open Settings and choose Release all protection, then confirm Release everything. This ends active sessions and switches off routines without a timer or purchase. If you cannot open Steer, revoke its access in iPhone Settings > Screen Time > Apps with Screen Time Access. Menu wording can vary by iOS version.

Routines and early exits

Routines follow the times and days you choose; morning routines do not detect when you wake up. Pausing one protected period does not stop other active routines. Commitment pauses add friction before an early exit; they are not an unbreakable lock.

Subscriptions

When Steer Pro is available, the app displays Apple's current plans, prices and any eligible trial. Purchases are processed by Apple. Use Restore purchases in Steer's Settings with the same Apple Account. Manage or cancel a subscription in Apple's subscription settings. Deleting Steer does not cancel a subscription. If plans cannot load, the app will say so and free features remain available.

History and privacy

Recorded focus is time protected by Steer, not a measurement of screen time saved. Overlapping blocks count once. In Settings you can export session history or clear completed history. Clearing history does not cancel a subscription. Read the privacy policy for details.
